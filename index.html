About NOVA AI STUDIO

NOVA AI STUDIO is an online text-to-image tool designed to help people turn written ideas into visual creations. Users can describe a scene, choose an image style and generate an image using artificial intelligence.

Our goal is to make AI image creation simple and accessible. The tool is designed for beginners, creators, students and anyone interested in exploring creative ideas.

Image generation is provided through an external AI service. Availability and usage may depend on the service's limits.
