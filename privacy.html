Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026 · Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how NOVA AI STUDIO handles information when you visit the website, create an account, or use its available creative tools.

1. Information we use

Depending on the features you use, NOVA may process:

  • Account information: your name, email address, authentication details and account identifiers when you register or sign in with email or Google.
  • Content you submit: prompts, story text and reference images that you choose to send to an AI tool.
  • Basic technical information: information that hosting, authentication and infrastructure providers may process to deliver, protect and troubleshoot the service, such as request and security logs.
  • Browser storage: project and story drafts saved by the current website may be stored in your browser's local storage on that device. They are not automatically synced to your account.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and maintain accounts, authenticate users, provide requested features, process submitted prompts or images, protect the service against abuse, troubleshoot errors and improve the website.

3. Service providers

NOVA relies on third-party services to operate. These currently include:

  • Supabase: account authentication and related account services.
  • Google: Google sign-in when you choose that option. Google's own privacy terms also apply to its services.
  • Cloudflare: website infrastructure and AI processing for supported generation features.
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting.

When you use a feature, relevant information may be processed by the provider needed to deliver that feature. Those providers handle information under their own policies and terms. NOVA does not control their independent practices.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers described above, or disclosed when reasonably necessary to protect the service, comply with applicable law, enforce these terms, or respond to a valid legal request.

5. Storage and security

Account information is handled through Supabase. Drafts saved with browser local storage remain on that browser unless you clear the browser's site data. AI prompts and reference images are sent to the relevant generation service when you request generation. No online service can guarantee absolute security, and you should not submit passwords, financial details, highly sensitive personal information or confidential material to creative tools.

6. Retention and deletion

Information may be retained for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain account security, meet legal obligations or resolve disputes, subject to the retention practices of our providers. You can clear locally saved drafts through your browser settings. For account deletion or a privacy request, use the support/contact channel provided by NOVA AI STUDIO. Some records may need to be retained where the law permits or requires it.

7. Children

NOVA AI STUDIO is not designed for children under 13, and children should not create accounts or submit personal information without the involvement and permission of a parent or legal guardian. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the service operator through the website's support channel.

8. International processing

Because NOVA and its service providers may operate in different countries, information may be processed outside the country where you live, subject to the providers' practices and applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website and its features change. The latest version and its effective date will be published on this page. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the support/contact channel made available on the NOVA AI STUDIO website. A direct support email should be added here before the service is opened to the public.
